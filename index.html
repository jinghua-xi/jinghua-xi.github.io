About me

I am a master’s student in mathematics at the University of Bonn. My interests lie in partial differential equations, functional analysis, and probability theory.

Previously, I studied mathematics in the Fields Honors Program at the Southern University of Science and Technology. My research has concerned self-similarity and the geometry of continuous functions.

I am seeking working student positions or internships in mathematical optimization, scientific computing, and Python-based data analysis in the Bonn–Cologne area.

Research

Graphs of continuous but non-affine functions are never self-similar

C. G. Moreira, J. Xi, and Y. Zhang

Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society 153 (2025), 2501–2512.

We prove that the graph of a continuous function on [0, 1] is self-similar if and only if it is a straight line.

Mathematical outreach

Founder & organizer · 2023–2025

Undergraduate Mathematics Salon

I founded the mathematics salon at SUSTech in autumn 2023, inspired by an observation from organizing discussion groups: the speaker often learns the most. Open to all undergraduates, the salon gave students a place to explain ideas, work through proofs, and learn from one another.

The salon was featured on the university’s official WeChat account. Its static archive preserves 28 recorded talks from 2023–2024, together with available abstracts, notes, and photographs.
