Graphs of continuous but non-affine functions are never self-similar

Jinghua Xi · 12 Dec 2023


Abstract:

We show a rigidity result about self similarity for the graph of a continuous function. To be more precise, we show that the graph of a one-variable continuous function on a closed interval is self-similar if and only if the function is affine.
