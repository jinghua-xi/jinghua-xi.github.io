Topics on Modular Forms

Shenguanlin Lin · 28 Mar 2024

Abstract

In the next 3 weeks we will concentrate on developing some formulas to compute the dimension for the vector spaces Mk(r) of modular forms. Sheaf cohomology is an important tool to derive Riemann-Roch theorem, which gives one of the most important formulas. Cech cohomology, which is much easier to compute, can be used as an approximation to the sheaf cohomology.
