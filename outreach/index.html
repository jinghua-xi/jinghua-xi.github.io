SUSTech · Sharing mathematics

Mathematical outreach

I enjoy creating opportunities for students to explain ideas, ask questions, and learn mathematics together. These activities bring together my work as an organizer and a speaker.

Founder & organizer · 2023–2025

Undergraduate Mathematics Salon

I founded the mathematics salon at SUSTech in autumn 2023, inspired by an observation from organizing discussion groups: the speaker often learns the most. Open to all undergraduates, the salon gave students a place to explain ideas, work through proofs, and learn from one another.

The salon was featured on the university’s official WeChat account. Its static archive preserves 28 recorded talks from 2023–2024, together with available abstracts, notes, and photographs.

Co-organizer & speaker · 19–29 June 2024

Mathematics and Physics Society Workshop

I co-organized the first undergraduate-initiated Mathematics and Physics Society Workshop at SUSTech. The event brought together more than 600 participants overall.

I also gave a talk, “From the Riemann Sphere to Riemann Surface Theory,” for an audience joining in person and online.
